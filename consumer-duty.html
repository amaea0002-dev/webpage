FCA Consumer Duty

Four outcomes.
Ongoing proof.

Consumer Duty (effective July 2023) raised the bar for every FCA-regulated firm. It requires more than good practice: it requires documented evidence, across four outcome areas, continuously.

The four outcomes

What the FCA expects.

Firms must demonstrate good consumer outcomes in every area, not once at onboarding, but as an ongoing evidenced practice across every client relationship.

Products & Services

Products and services must be designed for, and actually meet the needs of a defined target market. Suitability must be demonstrated at every interaction, not assumed at onboarding.

Price & Value

The FCA requires firms to assess and evidence that charges are fair and proportionate to the value delivered. Ongoing fees must be justified by ongoing service, documented, not assumed.

Consumer Understanding

Clients must genuinely understand what they're receiving, not just sign a document. Firms must evidence that their communications are clear, timely, and effective for their specific client base.

Consumer Support

Clients must be able to access the help they need without friction or unreasonable barriers. Firms must monitor and evidence that support is accessible, particularly for vulnerable clients.
