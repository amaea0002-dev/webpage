Sound familiar?

The adviser did everything right.
There's just no record of it.

4pm. Thursday.

The FCA wants evidence by Friday. You open the tracker.

Annual review due: 04/31/30004

You stare at it. You move on.

Dave Hill. Annual review — blank.

Not overdue. Not missing. Just blank. You go to the adviser. She did that review. Months ago. An hour with Dave. She did everything right.

There's just no record of it.

You check:

SharePointCRMSpreadsheet 1Spreadsheet 2

Seven Dave Hills. Three with the same adviser. No date of birth. No NI number. Nothing to tell them apart.

She finds her notes. Types them in. Hits save.

Excel crashes.

Types them in again. Column empty. Twenty minutes. A review that happened. A client who was helped. No proof it ever took place.

"The FCA doesn't care that it happened.
They care that you can prove it."

This isn't unusual. It's every firm, every week.

Three ways the same problem shows up every day:

Annual reviews slipping past deadlines

With hundreds of clients across multiple advisers, it's nearly impossible to track every 12-month window manually. Breaches accumulate quietly until the FCA visit.

Consumer Duty documentation scattered and incomplete

Vulnerable client records, fair value assessments, and suitability letters spread across Intelliflo, SharePoint, and email — impossible to audit in one place.

Regulatory reporting consuming your team's time

RMAR submissions, Consumer Duty assessments, and board-level compliance reports take days of manual data gathering — time that should be spent on clients.
