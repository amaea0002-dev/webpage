Why we built this.

Because compliance shouldn't depend on whether the right person remembers where Robert Chen's suitability report lives. Or which Excel sheet logged a review date as 04/31/3036.

Our story

Amaea didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a spreadsheet - one that had a client's annual review date logged as 04/31/3036.

Hasna spent several years in financial services doing the work that compliance software was supposed to make easy: manually pulling data for Consumer Duty reports and board packs, trawling through folders to check whether a suitability report had been signed, cross-referencing dates across disconnected systems, and entering it all into a manual Excel sheet that nobody fully trusted.

The problem wasn't that firms were non-compliant. It was that they were doing everything right - and couldn't prove it. The work existed. The evidence didn't.

So Amaea was built around the compliance intelligence layer firms actually need: an AI that reads, classifies, and surfaces the evidence that was always there but never accessible. The work an experienced compliance officer would do if they had the time.

The result is a product that's easy to set up, easy to use, and actually solves the problem - not just makes it more visible. Amaea launches in 2028. We're currently working directly with IFA firms to make sure we get it right before anyone depends on it.
