About

Building the compliance platform
advisers deserve.

Amaea was founded after years of watching talented compliance teams drown in spreadsheets, missed reviews, and manual documentation. There's a better way.

Our story

Every IFA firm we spoke to had the same problem: their compliance team spent more time chasing documentation than actually protecting clients. Annual reviews slipped. Suitability letters expired. Consumer Duty added pressure without adding tools.

We built Amaea to change that. A compliance platform that tracks every client journey, surfaces gaps before they become breaches, and generates the reports your team needs, automatically.

Amaea launches in 2028. We're currently in the build and validation phase, working directly with IFA firms to make sure we get it right.

What we believe

Compliance should protect clients,
not consume teams.

Evidence-first

Every feature we build starts with a documented adviser need, not a tech idea. We talk to compliance teams before we write a line of code.

Proportionate

Small IFA firms face the same FCA scrutiny as large ones. Our pricing and tooling reflect that: no enterprise bloat, no hidden costs.

Transparent

We tell you what Amaea can and can't do. No overpromising. No fabricated stats. Just an honest platform that does what it says.
