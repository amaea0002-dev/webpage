Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

Amaea is a trading name of Amaea Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. We operate as a data processor for the personal data of your clients, and as a data controller for the data of your firm's users.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact details of firm users (name, work email, role)
  • Firm details (name, FCA reference number, adviser structure)
  • Client compliance records imported via Intelliflo and SharePoint integrations
  • Usage and access logs

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the Amaea platform
  • To generate compliance reports and alerts
  • To improve the platform (aggregated, anonymised only)
  • We never sell data or use it for advertising purposes

4. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, restrict, and delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@amaea.co.uk.

5. Data retention

Firm and user data is retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days. Client compliance records are retained as configured by your firm administrator.

6. Contact

For privacy-related queries, contact us at privacy@amaea.co.uk.
