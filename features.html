Client Journey Tracking

Every client relationship has three compliance stages: Initial Engagement, Ad-hoc Work, and Annual Reviews. Amaea maps the complete journey for every client — flagging exactly where documentation is missing or overdue.

  • Stage-by-stage compliance visibility for every client in your book
  • Milestone tracking: fact find, suitability reports, client agreements, AML checks
  • Colour-coded status: Compliant, At Risk, Overdue, Breach
  • Click any milestone to review the document checklist instantly
  • Adviser-level compliance view — see performance across your entire team

Consumer Duty Monitoring

Consumer Duty isn't a one-off exercise — it requires ongoing evidence of fair outcomes across every client interaction. Amaea automates the monitoring so you always know exactly where you stand.

  • Automated vulnerable client identification and enhanced monitoring
  • Real-time Consumer Duty health score across four FCA outcome areas
  • Fair value assessment tracking and documentation status
  • One-click Consumer Duty annual assessment report for the board
  • Escalation workflows for high-risk clients requiring immediate action
