Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026 · Version 1.0

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. They can remember preferences (like your dark-mode choice), keep you signed in, or measure how the site is used. We're required by UK PECR + GDPR to be clear about which ones we set and why.

2. Cookies we currently set

On amaea.co.uk (this marketing site), we set zero non-essential cookies. Specifically:

  • amaea-theme (localStorage, essential): remembers your dark / light mode choice. Doesn't leave your browser.
  • amaea-cookie-consent (localStorage, essential): remembers your response to the cookie banner so we don't ask again.

We don't run analytics, advertising, or tracking on this site at this stage. If we add any in future, the cookie banner will ask for your consent before they load.

3. The Amaea app (app.amaea.co.uk)

The product itself uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in (Supabase auth) and to remember your interface preferences. These are required to deliver the service; under PECR they don't require explicit consent. Full detail is in the in-app Settings → Privacy section once you're signed in.

4. Sub-processor cookies

A handful of third parties may set cookies on browser sessions that hit specific surfaces (e.g. Cloudflare for DDoS protection, Sentry for error correlation on the app). These are operational, not advertising. Full list with regions is on our Security & data handling page.

5. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Clearing the two preference cookies above means you'll see the cookie banner again and lose your dark-mode choice; everything else still works. We don't track you across the web, so there's nothing else to opt out of.

6. Changes to this policy

If we start using new cookies (e.g. analytics post-launch), we'll update this page and the cookie banner will re-prompt for your consent on next visit. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the most recent change.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies, tracking, or our privacy posture: privacy@amaea.co.uk.
